What we built

Upload a PDF, get a short list of warning signs with page numbers

1

Read

Pull the text out of the PDF, clean it, and split it into sentences. Each sentence keeps its page number.

2

Score

A trained AI model gives every sentence a risk score from 0 to 1 and a type: Financial, Legal, Regulatory, Operational or Business.

3

Filter

Remove standard template text. Mark the most serious signs, like going concern or loan default, as “severe”.

4

Show

A dashboard lists the findings, most serious first, with the page to check and a short summary.

In simple words: it works like a highlighter pen that has read thousands of examples of what a warning sign looks like.

3 · The analyst still makes the final decision. The tool decides what they should read first.

This is what we built, in four steps. First, read: we take the PDF, pull out the text, clean it and break it into sentences, and every sentence remembers its page. Second, score: a trained AI model looks at each sentence and says how risky it is, from 0 to 1, and what type of risk it is. Third, filter: annual reports have a lot of standard template text that sounds scary but is not, so we push that down, and we mark the really serious signs, like going concern doubts or loan defaults, as severe. Fourth, show: a dashboard lists the findings, most serious first, with page numbers. Think of it as a smart highlighter pen. It does not replace the analyst, it tells them where to look first.
