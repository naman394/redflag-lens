M1 · Users and business objective

Built for the credit analyst at a bank or NBFC

Primary user

Credit analyst

Reviews borrowers’ annual reports for loan appraisal and yearly renewal. Needs every warning sign, with proof.

Secondary users

Risk and EWS teams

Early-warning-signal monitoring units, internal auditors, and equity or bond analysts screening many companies.

Why now

RBI requires it

RBI’s 2024 Fraud Risk Management Directions require banks to run an early-warning-signal framework, and a red flag needs documented evidence.

Business objective

Cut the first-pass review of an annual report from hours to minutes, and miss fewer red flags, with every finding linked to its page.

3

Our user is specific: the credit analyst at a bank or NBFC. The business driver is regulatory. RBI’s Master Directions on Fraud Risk Management (July 2024) require banks to have an early-warning-signal framework, and a borrower must be heard before an account is tagged as fraud, so every flag needs evidence. That is why our output always cites the sentence and page. Mention that we plan to interview two or three analysts to confirm current review time; the time-saving numbers are targets until then.
