Back in milestone one we wrote down targets before building anything, so we could not move the goalposts later. Here is the scorecard. Finding risk sentences: goal 0.80, we got 0.86. Catching real risks: goal 0.85, we got 0.92. Naming the right risk type: goal 0.70, FinBERT gets exactly 0.70. Time per report on a laptop: goal three minutes, FinBERT takes about two and a half. Separating failed companies from healthy ones: goal 0.75, we got 0.94. One target is open: checking the AI summary, because our Gemini credit ran out. There is a trade-off. DeBERTa finds more risks but is weaker at naming the type, 0.49. FinBERT is better at types and fast on a laptop. So the laptop app uses FinBERT, and on a GPU server we use DeBERTa, which takes about five seconds per report.
