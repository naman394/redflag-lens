The riskiest sections are the smallest

Auditor’s report, CARO, contingent liabilities and secretarial audit together are under 11% of all sentences.

But they hold the strongest red flags: going-concern doubts, defaults, qualified opinions, penalties.

Decision: oversample these sections when labelling, and weight them in the product.
