To sum up. RedFlag Lens reads an entire annual report and puts the warning signs on top, with the page to check. On companies it never saw, it scores 0.86 F1 and finds 92 percent of real risks. On ten brand-new annual reports, a failed company ranks above a healthy one 96 percent of the time, and on the final clean exam every time. On our 8-report backtest it is 0.94. And it runs on a normal laptop in about two and a half minutes, or five seconds on a GPU. It does not replace the analyst; it saves them hours and points them to the right pages.
