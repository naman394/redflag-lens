16 · Test set will be labelled by people only, with no LLM suggestions

Leakage is the classic mistake here. Companies reuse large parts of their reports year to year; we measured 27.7% verbatim reuse for DHFL. Company names are among the most distinctive words, so a sentence-level random split would let the model recognise companies instead of risk language. Every company sits in exactly one split, and Gensol is held out completely for the final backtest demo.
