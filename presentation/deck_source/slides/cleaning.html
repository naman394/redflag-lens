These are real issues we hit, not textbook steps. The rupee one is the most interesting: older Indian reports draw ₹ with a special font, so plain text extraction turns it into a backtick or the letter H, which would break amount extraction later. We also caught a bug in our own deduplication: it removed sentences repeated across two years of the same company, which would have broken the year-over-year feature. It now removes repeats only within a report.
