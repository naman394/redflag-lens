Here is the problem. Before a bank lends money to a company, someone has to read its annual report. These reports are long, often more than 250 pages. Out of all those sentences, only about one in a hundred is a real warning sign, like a missed loan payment, a court case or an auditor saying something is wrong. The rest is normal text. A good example is Gensol Engineering, which collapsed in 2025. In its 2024 report, on page 187, the auditor already gave an adverse remark. The warning was there, it was just buried. Our project tries to find those buried sentences automatically.
