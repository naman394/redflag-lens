Keyword search can’t tell boilerplate from a red flag

“Going concern” appears in every healthy report: auditors write it in standard wording.

Only “qualified opinion” separates the groups cleanly.

This is why we add a Specific vs Generic label and train a model, instead of matching keywords.

13

This is our most important M2 finding. Standard auditor text says things like “if we conclude that a material uncertainty exists in relation to going concern…” in every report, so a keyword search flags all 16 healthy companies. The words are the same; what differs is whether the sentence describes a concrete event about this company. That is exactly what our Specific vs Generic label captures, and why the project needs a trained classifier rather than rules.
