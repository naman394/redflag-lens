M3 · Making it better

Four changes took the best score from 0.77 to 0.86

A bigger, stronger model

Moved from FinBERT to DeBERTa-v3-large, trained on rented GPUs (RunPod). We ran 36 training runs and picked the winner on validation only.

Extra “not a risk” examples

Added 600 real sentences that sound scary but are safe, such as “no fraud was noticed” and standard auditor wording.

Average of 3 copies

Trained the same model 3 times with different random starts and averaged the answers. Like asking three experts instead of one.

A severe tier and a template filter

A fixed list of the most serious phrases (going concern, default, fraud, NCLT) goes to the top. Text repeated across 3+ companies is pushed down.

8 · We also doubled the test set from 300 to 600 sentences so the models could be told apart fairly.

How did we improve from FinBERT’s 0.77 to 0.86? Four things. One: a bigger model, DeBERTa-v3-large, trained on rented GPUs from RunPod. We tried 36 training runs and chose the winner using only the validation set, never the test. Two: we added 600 extra examples of sentences that sound scary but are safe, for example “no fraud was noticed” or standard auditor wording. This taught the model not to panic at certain words. Three: we trained the same model three times and averaged them, like asking three experts and taking their average opinion. Four: after the model, we added a severe tier, a fixed list of the most serious phrases like going concern, loan default, fraud or insolvency court, written before we ran the final tests. We also push down template text that appears in three or more companies’ reports. And we doubled the test set so the comparison is fairer.
