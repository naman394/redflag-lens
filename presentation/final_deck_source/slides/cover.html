Good morning. Our project is RedFlag Lens. In one line: you give it a company’s annual report as a PDF, and it reads every sentence and gives you a short list of warning signs, things like loan defaults, auditor complaints or court cases, each one with the page it came from, so an analyst can check it in seconds. These four numbers are the story of the talk: we used 26 real annual reports, about 97 thousand sentences, our best model gets an F1 of 0.86, and on real companies it separates the ones that failed from the healthy ones with a score of 0.94 out of 1.
