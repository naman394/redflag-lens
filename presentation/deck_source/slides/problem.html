Jet Airways FY2018 · auditor’s report · page 84

“The appropriateness of assumption of going concern is dependent upon realization of the various initiatives undertaken by the Company and/or the Company’s ability to raise requisite finance…”

Published in 2018. The airline was grounded in April 2019. Indian reports have no single risk section: warnings sit in the auditor’s report, the CARO annexure, notes and the secretarial audit.

2

The core problem: the information is public, but it is buried. An average report in our corpus is 319 pages and about 3,700 sentences, and only about 5% of random sentences are risk statements. The Jet Airways example is real, extracted by our pipeline from page 84 of the FY2018 report: the auditor flagged going-concern doubt a year before the airline was grounded. Unlike US 10-K filings, Indian reports have no single “Risk Factors” section, so you have to know where to look.
