12 · Red-flag phrases counted with keyword rules (going concern, default, insolvency, qualified opinion, penalty, fraud, downgrade)

A first statistical test with simple keyword rules. Distressed reports have more than twice the red-flag phrase density of healthy ones, and the difference is significant at p = 0.016. But look at the overlap: keyword counting ranks Bajaj Finance, a healthy NBFC, above several distressed companies, and misses Jet Airways and Gensol. That motivates the next slide.
