To sum up. RedFlag Lens reads an entire annual report and puts the warning signs on top, with the page to check. On companies it never saw, it scores 0.86 F1 and finds 92 percent of real risks. On the five whole reports it never used, every failed company ranks above every healthy one; with the three validation companies added it is 0.94 on eight. On two brand-new reports it found 8 real problems for SpiceJet, with all 25 severe flags correct, and none for Nestlé. And it runs on a normal laptop in about two and a half minutes, or five seconds on a GPU. It does not replace the analyst; it saves them hours and points them to the right pages.
