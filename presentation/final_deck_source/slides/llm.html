Examiners often ask: why not just paste the report into ChatGPT? We want to be honest here. We did not test GPT or ChatGPT. We tested an open model, Qwen 2.5 with 7 billion parameters, without any training, and it scored 0.56, well below our trained model. A much bigger model might do better, and that is on our next-steps list. But there are three reasons our approach still makes sense. First, trust: our model only scores sentences that really exist in the PDF, so every page number is real. Chatbots can invent quotes. Second, privacy and cost: it runs on a laptop or inside the bank, so no data leaves, and there is no fee per report. We only use an LLM for the final summary, and a checker removes any claim that is not supported by a found sentence.
