10 · Two-step model · severe list v2 (RBI early-warning signs), checked on training companies only

To test the tool on truly new material we used ten annual reports that were never in any of our data. After the first six, we improved the severe warning list using RBI's early-warning signs, such as lenders selling pledged shares or a breach of loan conditions, and we taught it that a merger approved at the NCLT is normal business. Because those six had shown us the mistakes, we treated them as practice. We checked the new rules only on training companies, froze them, and then downloaded four new reports as a final exam. Reliance Infrastructure scored 20 severe signs per thousand sentences: the auditor could not even give an opinion on its accounts. Sintex scored 10, with loan defaults and a credit rating cut to D, and all 23 flags were real. The two healthy companies, Dabur and Hero, scored below one. So every failed company ranks above every healthy one. Across all ten new reports the score is 0.96. The one miss is Future Retail: its report was written before it defaulted, so the danger words were not there yet. That is our next challenge, early warning.
